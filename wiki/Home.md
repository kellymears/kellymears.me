A personal reference wiki: one note per concept, each written to stand on its own, densely linked to the others.

The scope is **general knowledge** — ideas that hold outside any particular codebase, employer, or project. Nothing here documents a specific application. Where a note describes a hazard or a technique in concrete terms, the concreteness is there to make the idea usable, not to describe a particular system.

Notes are short by design. If something needs more room, it becomes several notes.
