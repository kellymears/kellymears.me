**Astroturfing** is manufactured grassroots support: a campaign engineered to look like spontaneous, independent agreement from ordinary people when it is in fact organised, funded, or scripted by a single interested party. A **front group** supplies the structure — a name like a citizens' coalition that discloses neither its funder nor its message's origin. Paid commenters, bot networks, and coordinated form-letter campaigns supply the volume, standing in for participants a genuine movement would have organised itself.

The tells are fairly consistent. **Sudden volume** — comments, calls, or reviews arriving in a tight window with no visible organising history — is one; genuine movements usually leave a longer trail. **Template language** is another: independent people write in their own words, while manufactured campaigns reuse a script closely enough that overlapping phrasing across many "different" authors gives it away. **Shallow histories** are a third: accounts or organisations that appear right before the campaign and produce nothing before or after it.

Astroturfing works because it exploits a real and usually reliable heuristic: if many independent people reach the same position without coordinating, that convergence is itself evidence the position is worth taking seriously. Genuine [[Collective Action]] produces exactly this signature honestly — a threshold of independent people is the whole point of a pledge campaign. Astroturfing counterfeits the signature without the underlying convergence, which makes it a [[Confidence Trick]] rather than ordinary persuasion: the audience is not fooled about the content, but about who holds the position and how many of them there are. Attributing every unwelcome surge of activity to astroturfing is itself a [[Plausible Mechanism]] move unless the funding can actually be traced — genuine movements do occasionally arrive at sudden, real consensus.

Its target is often the [[Overton Window]] rather than any single vote — manufactured volume can make a fringe position look like an existing, arguable-with constituency, which pairs naturally with a manufactured [[Moral Panic]], where synthetic volume supplies apparent urgency as well as apparent consensus.
