**Hyperfocus** is a state of intense, long-lasting concentration on an engaging task, often with reduced awareness of everything else: hunger, time, people talking, the meeting that started twenty minutes ago. It is widely reported by people with ADHD and by autistic people. It sounds paradoxical for a condition named for attention *deficit*, and the paradox dissolves once ADHD is understood as a difficulty *regulating* attention rather than having too little of it. Attention that can't easily be directed also can't easily be withdrawn.

Hyperfocus is less studied than its prominence suggests. Reviews describe its features fairly consistently but note that measurement is immature, so much of what is known comes from self-report. The commonly described features:

- **It attaches to interest, not importance.** The task that grips is the novel, rewarding, or puzzling one, which is often not the one due tomorrow. That makes it unreliable as a productivity strategy.
- **Exit is the hard part.** Stopping, switching, or even noticing the time is difficult, a direct cost of weak [[Executive Function|executive]] control over transitions. [[Time Blindness]] makes it worse.
- **The body gets ignored.** Signals from [[Interoception]], such as hunger, thirst, and needing the bathroom, are muted or overridden for hours, and the bill arrives afterwards.
- **It can be enormously productive.** Much of what hyperfocus produces could not have been done in fragments.

The [[Flow]] comparison is natural and partly misleading. Flow arises from a match between challenge and skill and tends to leave people refreshed. Hyperfocus arises from a person's attentional profile, can happen during low-challenge activities such as scrolling or reorganizing a configuration, and often leaves people depleted. [[Monotropism]] offers an explanation for both its depth and its stickiness: attention concentrated into a single deep channel.

Living well with hyperfocus is mostly about the edges. External timers and alarms, water and food within reach, and a written note of where to resume all help. So does choosing what to start in the first place, since the start is often the only point where there is a choice. A [[Rabbit Hole|rabbit hole]] entered on purpose is a feature. One fallen into at 4 p.m. on deadline day is a [[Yak Shaving|yak]].
