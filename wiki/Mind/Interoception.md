**Interoception** is the sense of the body's internal state: hunger, fullness, thirst, heartbeat, breathlessness, temperature, fatigue, the need for the bathroom. It is sometimes called the eighth sense, alongside the familiar five, [[Proprioception]] (where the body is), and the [[Vestibular System]] (balance and motion). Signals arrive from the organs, are integrated largely in the insular cortex, and become the felt states that tell a person what their body needs.

People differ a lot in interoceptive accuracy, and the differences matter. Lab tasks such as counting one's own heartbeats without touching a pulse show wide variation, with lower and less consistent accuracy reported, on average, in autistic people and in people with ADHD. Low interoceptive awareness means signals arrive faint, late, or ambiguous:

- **Hunger and thirst** go unnoticed until they become headache, irritability, or shakiness, or are noticed only when [[Hyperfocus]] breaks.
- **Overheating and fatigue** are missed until they become urgent, so a person runs past the point where a break would have been easy. [[Thermoregulation]] works, but the conscious warning lags behind it.
- **Pain** is under- or over-registered, a separate route from [[Nociception]] proper.

Interoception also underlies emotion. Prominent theories of emotion hold that feelings are partly the brain's interpretation of bodily states: a racing heart, a tight stomach. A blurry interoceptive signal makes emotions hard to identify, and *alexithymia*, difficulty recognizing and naming one's own feelings, overlaps heavily with low interoceptive awareness. The same arousal can be read as excitement or as anxiety depending on context, which is part of why the [[Placebo Effect]] and the [[Nocebo Effect]] work at all.

As with [[Time Blindness]], the remedies are external. Scheduled meals and water, alarms for breaks, and a deliberate check of body state ("when did I last eat?") all compensate. Interoceptive training through attention exercises shows some promise. Meanwhile, routines carry what the inner sense doesn't, a form of [[Defensive Default]] for the body.
