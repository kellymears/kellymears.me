**Working memory** is the mental workspace that holds information while it is being used: a phone number between reading it and dialing it, the earlier clauses of a sentence while parsing the rest, the three things to check before submitting. It differs from long-term memory in two ways. It is tiny, and it decays within seconds unless rehearsed.

How tiny has been revised downward. George Miller's 1956 "magical number seven, plus or minus two" became folklore. Later work, notably Nelson Cowan's, puts the real limit at **about four chunks** once rehearsal and grouping tricks are controlled for. A *chunk* is whatever the mind treats as a unit. That is why expertise looks like a bigger working memory: a chess master holds a board position as a few familiar patterns, while a novice holds twenty-odd separate pieces. Expertise changes what fits in a slot, not the number of slots.

The influential model (Baddeley and Hitch, 1974, later extended) splits working memory into a verbal store (the *phonological loop*, the inner voice repeating a number), a visual-spatial store, and a *central executive* that directs attention between them. That executive role is why working memory counts as a core [[Executive Function]]. Losing hold of the plan mid-task is not a memory failure in the everyday sense. The plan never made it out of a store that holds four things.

Design consequences show up everywhere:

- **Interfaces** that make people carry information from one screen to another overload working memory. [[Progressive Disclosure]] and keeping relevant information visible are working-memory accommodations. See also [[Cognitive Load]].
- **Code** that requires tracking many variables, flags, or levels of indirection at once is hard to read for this reason. Good [[Naming]] works as chunking.
- **Interruptions** wipe the workspace, and rebuilding it is much of the cost of [[Attention Residue]].

There is a close analogy in machines. A [[Context Window]] is a language model's working memory, with the same consequence: what isn't in it doesn't exist for the computation, and moving things out to durable notes ([[Agent Memory]]) is the remedy for people and models alike.
