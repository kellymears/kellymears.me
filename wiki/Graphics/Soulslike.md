**Soulslike** describes the design lineage descending from FromSoftware's *Demon's Souls* and *Dark Souls*, characterised less by difficulty itself than by what the difficulty is for. The wager is that an obstacle which cannot be trivially overcome produces a stronger relationship to the space beyond it. Difficulty is treated as authorship — a claim about what the work is, not a slider left in the wrong position.

The wager only pays out under a strict condition: **failure has to be legible**. A player must be able to reconstruct why they died and what would have prevented it. This is why the genre's combat is built on long, readable animation tells, why attacks commit the attacker to a recovery window, and why enemy placement is fixed rather than generated. A death caused by an unreadable tell, an off-screen attacker, or a variable frame rate is noise, and noise breaks the contract. The genre depends on [[Determinism]] in the same way a [[Reproducible Case]] does: an unrepeatable failure teaches nothing. Where [[Roguelike]] design accepts randomness and asks the player to adapt, this lineage removes it so blame lands squarely.

**The bonfire structure** is the mechanism that makes repeated failure survivable. Checkpoints restore the world along with the player, converting a lost attempt into a bounded cost rather than an open one — a form of [[Idempotence]] applied to a play session. Currency dropped on death and recoverable at the spot where it fell adds a second, optional stake without touching the first, and this is where the design comes nearest to [[Terror Management Theory]]: the game makes dying routine and then asks what you are willing to risk anyway.

The lineage is frequently misread as a case for hostility toward players. It is not. The commitments that make it work — consistent [[Game Feel]], honest tells, no hidden state — are legibility commitments, and legibility is an [[Accessibility]] argument. Difficulty options and clear tells are orthogonal; treating them as a trade is a failure of [[Plausible Mechanism]], not a defence of the form. What is genuinely load-bearing is that the world teaches through arrangement rather than instruction, which puts [[Environmental Storytelling]] at the centre of the genre rather than at its edges.
