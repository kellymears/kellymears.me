**Rhythm Game** synchronises player input to a musical beat, judging each press against a timing window and awarding tiers of accuracy — commonly Perfect, Great, Good and Miss — that determine score and, in stricter titles, whether the run continues. The judgement is a [[Skill Check]] repeated dozens of times a song, its window narrow enough to be argued in [[Frame Data]] terms, and the genre stands or falls on how legible that check feels: a hit that is correct but reads late is worse than a hit that is wrong but reads fair, which is why it leans on [[Game Feel]] harder than almost any other form.

**Offset calibration is not a settings-menu nicety, it is fairness.** Audio and display latency vary by hardware — headphones, TVs, Bluetooth — and an uncalibrated setup can shift a player's true timing by tens of milliseconds relative to what the chart expects, quietly failing hits a well-tuned rig would pass. Titles serious about the genre treat calibration as [[Accessibility]] work, alongside adjustable window sizes offered as [[Assist Mode]] for players whose reaction time or motor control does not match the default.

**Charts**, the authored map of which input lands on which beat, are still mostly hand-built by ear despite attempts at algorithmic generation from raw audio, because a good chart interprets a song's feel rather than its onsets; [[Procedural Generation]] gets close on simple tracks and badly wrong on syncopated ones. Nearly all of these games play a pre-composed track and ask the player to meet it, including the ones that sound improvised: *Thumper* (2016, Drool) was scored by Brian Gibson level by level around fixed time signatures, and the player hits lit notes in time with music that is already there, adding percussion on top. *Tetris Effect* (2018) works the same way, layering effects over a composed backing. Genuinely generative audio is rarer — *Everyday Shooter* (2007) builds its guitar score out of the enemies the player destroys — but in either arrangement, chasing a clean run, a tight [[Compulsion Loop]] feeding full-combo [[Achievement Hunting]], starts to feel like composing.
