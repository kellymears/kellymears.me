**Creature Collector** describes games built around capturing wild creatures into a roster, then fielding a team in turn-based combat governed by a type-matchup chart — a rock-paper-scissors layer where each creature's element is strong or weak against others by design rather than by stats alone. Capture is the genre's signature mechanic and its central economy: a roster is built by attrition and chance, not unlocked from a fixed cast, so the game is structurally a [[Compulsion Loop]] of encountering, weakening and capturing before combat even starts.

**Breeding and stat inheritance** extend that collecting instinct past capture. Two creatures produce offspring that inherit and recombine hidden stat values, and competitive players breed for specific combinations much as a card player hunts a synergy, except the unit of assembly is [[Variable-Ratio Reinforcement]] applied to genetics, and an optimal specimen can take dozens of attempts. This is the genre's defining tension: collecting rewards catching everything, while competitive team-building rewards narrowing to a handful of optimal specimens and discarding the rest, and completionist pressure to fill every roster slot sits close enough to [[Gacha Monetization]]'s collection-gap psychology that the two are sometimes lumped together, though capture is normally gated by skill and encounter rate rather than a purchasable roll.

**The result reads as a treadmill by design.** Progress is measured in roster completion and in [[Meta-Progression]] carried between battles, and the competitive scene runs on [[Tier List]] rankings and a [[Metagame]] that shifts with every balance patch, much as a fighting game's does, with full-roster [[Achievement Hunting]] running alongside it as its own goal. *Temtem* (Crema; early access 2020, 1.0 in 2022) makes the loop explicitly social by building it into an MMO structure — a persistent, shared [[Live Service Game]] world where players trade with each other, though breeding stays a private operation on two creatures one already owns, carried out at a breeding centre — sharpening the tension between collecting and competing rather than resolving it.
