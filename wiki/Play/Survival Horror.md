**Survival Horror** is a design stance more than a fixed rule set: scarce resources, restricted mobility, and a player character built to be vulnerable rather than capable. Ammunition, healing items, and inventory space are kept shorter than a fight would comfortably require, so every encounter is first a resource-management decision — fight, flee, or waste something scarce finding out which was right — before it is a combat one. Bad mobility is deliberate, not a limitation: slow turns, no dodge, and fixed camera angles that hide threats are [[Game Feel]] choices in service of vulnerability, and later entries that streamlined movement had to rebuild scarcity elsewhere to keep the tension.

The genre has two ancestors, and they gave different things. [[Sweet Home]] (1989, Capcom), a Famicom horror RPG, supplied the mansion, permanent character death, the finite party inventory, puzzle locks, found documents, and a backtracking map structurally close to a [[Metroidvania]], since key items reopen earlier rooms rather than extend a line. The fixed cameras are not its bequest: they come from *Alone in the Dark* (1992), and Mikami has said *Resident Evil* would have been made in first person without it. *Resident Evil* (1996) combined the two on new hardware and has been the genre's throughline since: the finite item-box inventory as a [[Diegetic Interface]] the player physically manages, notes and corpses doing [[Environmental Storytelling]] instead of exposition, and enemy [[Game AI]] tuned to feel slow but relentless rather than clever.

**The series also carries the genre's central drift.** *Resident Evil 5* (2009) and *6* (2012) traded scarcity for firepower and mobility, chasing action-game audiences and drawing [[Ludonarrative Dissonance]] complaints — a horror story told through a power fantasy — before *7* and the remakes pulled scarcity back to the centre. *Resident Evil 4* (2005) is routinely swept into that account and does not belong: it still roots the player in place to aim, as 5 does, keeps ammunition tight, and turns storage into the attaché-case packing puzzle. Free movement while shooting arrives only with 6. Fixed save points, another deliberate friction, became fuel for [[Speedrun]] routing once players optimised the restrictions meant to slow them down.
