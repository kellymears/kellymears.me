**Roguelike Deckbuilder** is a hybrid genre, popularized by *Slay the Spire* (early access 2017, 1.0 in 2019, Mega Crit) and usually traced back through *Dream Quest* (2014), that keeps the [[Permadeath]] and [[Procedural Generation]] of the [[Roguelike]] but replaces its dungeon crawl with a deck built entirely within that run. Cards are acquired by **drafting** — choosing one reward from a small set after each encounter — so the deck itself is the character build, assembled from scratch every attempt rather than persisting between them. **Relics** (passive modifiers) and an **energy economy**, a hard per-turn budget on cards played, constrain what a deck can become, and most of a run's tension is spending relic slots and card picks on synergies that may not resolve before the deck meets a fight it cannot answer.

The genre departs from its parent in what progression means. A roguelike proper treats a run as one long branching decision under threat of loss, with skill as the only carryover; a deckbuilder treats the run as a small, closed puzzle re-solved from an empty deck each time, with [[Meta-Progression]] — permanent unlocks between runs — doing work a roguelike usually leaves to the player alone. The real content sits downstream of both: **synergy discovery**, the moment two mediocre cards multiply each other, is what a run is actually played for, and reward randomness functions as textbook [[Variable-Ratio Reinforcement]] inside a [[Compulsion Loop]] that resets to empty every attempt. That discovery becomes public fast; [[Tier List]] rankings chase a [[Metagame]] that shifts whenever a card's text changes.

*Balatro* (2024, LocalThunk, published by Playstack) pushes furthest from combat: there is no opponent to out-damage, only an escalating number to beat, so every joker is read purely for its effect on a chips-times-multiplier equation. It makes the puzzle comparable rather than private through [[Seeded Randomness]] and a set of preset Challenge decks, not through the daily-run format — that belongs to *Slay the Spire* and its Daily Climb.
